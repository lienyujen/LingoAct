01 / CLASSROOM

Four skills. One connected classroom.

Start with your course language and level, then choose activities, pronunciation support, and assessment that fit your goals.

L

Listening studio

Turn text or a screen capture into speech, then send a listening task or read-aloud practice with the pronunciation and annotation settings for your class.

S

Picture speaking and writing

Use an existing image or generate one with AI. Students receive the prompt, suggested vocabulary, and sentence patterns in the language they are learning.

R

Reading and quizzes

Create or adapt a passage at the selected level, with vocabulary, grammar notes, and comprehension questions. Reading tasks can stretch to i+1 or i+2.

W

Handwriting and composition

Students can write on the canvas, take a photo, upload an image, or paste their handwritten work. Teachers can review submissions and use AI feedback.

Other activities include vocabulary cards, polls, short answers, word clouds, discussion boards, random selection, buzz-in activities, exit tickets, and report exports.

02 / GET STARTED

From first launch to a complete lesson

Set up your own backend before you teach. Classroom data is stored in the Supabase project you configure.

  1. 01

    Get the right teacher edition

    Contact the author for the appropriate Windows or macOS edition. On Windows, extract the download and run the executable inside the folder. On macOS, open the DMG and move the app to Applications.

  2. 02

    Connect your Supabase project

    Enter your Supabase project URL or project reference and publishable key in Settings. If you have not set up the backend, use the in-app deployment wizard to create the tables and AI functions. Follow its prompts to supply an access token and the required AI keys.

  3. 03

    Choose a language, standard, and level

    Set the teaching language and proficiency level before opening the class. The Huayu edition defaults to Traditional Chinese and also supports Simplified Chinese. Simplified uses HSK and Putonghua; Traditional retains TBCL/TOCFL and the existing Taiwanese Mandarin settings. Individual tasks can use a different level.

  4. 04

    Invite students to scan and join

    Open the classroom QR code. Students join in a browser and enter their name. They can choose their interface language from the menu at the bottom right of the student page.

  5. 05

    Send an activity and watch responses

    Choose a listening, speaking, reading, or writing activity, prepare the material, and send it. Student pages update automatically. Picture prompts include vocabulary and sentence patterns; changing the interface language does not translate this practice material.

  6. 06

    Review feedback and save the results

    Review responses on the teacher dashboard and request analysis or grading when useful. Collect exit tickets, review the class report, and export results. Teachers should review AI feedback before using it for instruction.

Take your settings with you

On Windows, put .env beside the executable. On macOS, put it beside the app, outside the app bundle. Copy the folder to carry these public settings to another computer. Keep your .env when updating.

Include only the Supabase URL, public key, and student-page URL. Private Gemini and OpenAI keys stay in the backend. Configure the teacher management key separately. Replace the placeholders below with your own public settings.

VITE_SUPABASE_URL=https://YOUR_PROJECT.supabase.co
VITE_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY=YOUR_PUBLISHABLE_KEY
VITE_PUBLIC_APP_URL=https://lingo.ehuayu.org

Software licensing and cloud-service charges are separate. Supabase and AI allowances and costs depend on your own accounts and usage. Supabase · Gemini · OpenAI

03 / EDITIONS

Choose your teaching edition

All editions share the student page and activity data format. Their teacher interfaces, language standards, and activity configurations differ.

Huayu edition

Defaults to Traditional Chinese; also offers Simplified Chinese and English. Simplified uses HSK, Traditional uses TBCL/TOCFL, and English offers all three standards.

Simplified Chinese / HSK edition

Uses Simplified Chinese, HSK, and standard Mainland Putonghua. Students can still choose a translated interface.

NCACLS edition

A Traditional Chinese edition for Chinese schools, using the Huayu proficiency and pronunciation-annotation settings.

English teaching edition

Focuses on English teaching with appropriate proficiency settings and listening, speaking, reading, and writing activities.

Taiwanese language arts edition

For Chinese language arts as a first language in Taiwan, with curriculum stages and Bopomofo settings.

Full edition

Retains multiple teaching languages and activities for teachers who work across language courses.

Common classroom questions

Do students need to install an app?

No. Teachers use a desktop app; students join in a browser on a phone, tablet, or computer.

Does changing the interface language translate the Chinese practice?

Picture prompts, vocabulary, and sentence patterns stay in the teaching language. Some reading definitions, grammar notes, and assessment explanations are translated on demand when a student chooses a supported language. Chinese teaching references remain unchanged.

What if an activity does not appear?

Check that the teacher has finished sending it and that the student page is connected and up to date. Refresh once to load the latest page. If the problem continues, tell the teacher which activity it is and share the screen; avoid repeatedly submitting answers.

Can I use my own backend?

Yes. Configure your Supabase project. Join links carry the corresponding public connection information. Private AI keys should never appear on the student page or in .env.
